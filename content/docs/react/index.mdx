## Key Features

- **Five views**: Day, Week, Month, Year and Agenda, with an instant tab switch
- **Event management**: create, view, edit and delete events with a title, description, Subject, colour, start and end
- **Subjects**: Study, Work, Personal and Health to start with, plus your own; filter by Subject, and rename, delete or merge Subjects while their events follow along
- **Floating time picker**: shared by Create and Edit; hour, 5-minute step and any single minute, −/+ one minute, AM/PM in 12-hour mode; changing the start keeps the event's length
- **Phone-friendly time grid**: Week and Day scroll sideways on phones, with pinned day names and hour column; overlapping events share columns and every box stays complete
- **Month view text**: two-line chips in five-row months and multi-day bars with their full title
- **Tap an empty slot** in Week or Day view to create an event at that time
- **Drag & drop**: mouse on desktop, press and hold on touch screens; resize by dragging a box edge with the mouse
- **Saved on the device**: events, Subjects and settings are kept in `localStorage`
- **Settings**: dark mode, chip or dot event style, 12/24-hour format, the hour the day starts at, Agenda grouping, removing sample events
- **Single HTML build**: `npm run build:single` produces one offline file with the code, styles and font inside

## Core Concepts

### Events

An event has a title, an optional description, a start and end, a colour and a Subject. Events can be moved by drag and drop, or edited in the form. In Week and Day view, overlapping events are laid out side by side in columns.

### Subjects

Subjects group events, for example by school subject or by area of life. The Subject manager (Filter → **Edit / merge**, or Settings → **Subjects → Manage**) renames, deletes and merges them. Deleting asks whether to move the events to another Subject or keep them as "No subject". A backup of the previous Subject list is kept in `localStorage`.

### Views

- **Day** and **Week**: a time grid that scrolls up and down, and sideways on phones
- **Month**: chips per day, multi-day bars, and **+N more** when a day is full
- **Year**: twelve months at a glance; tap a month to open it
- **Agenda**: a searchable list, grouped by date or by colour

### Storage

There is no server or account. Everything is saved in the browser's `localStorage` for the address the calendar was opened from, so the single file, a hosted copy and the Next.js app keep separate data.
